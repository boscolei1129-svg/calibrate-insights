    GETTING STARTEDGet your Instagram export+
    1. In Instagram, open Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions.
    2. Choose Export your information (sometimes called Download your information), then choose your Instagram profile and export to your device.
    3. Select Followers and following, set the date range to All time, and choose JSON (recommended) or HTML.
    4. When Instagram makes the download available, download and unzip it. Open connections/followers_and_following (the folder name can vary).
    5. Use Choose files above. Select following.json and all followers_*.json files together. HTML files work too. Add any missing parts with Choose files.

    Use one account and one export per snapshot. Don’t mix unrelated exports. Instagram’s menu labels may vary.

    PRIVATE BY DESIGNYour data stays yours+

    Your selected files are read locally in this browser tab. Calibrate has no backend, analytics, cookies, tracking pixels, external fonts, or Instagram sign-in. The app blocks outgoing data connections.

    Lists live in memory only. Refresh, close the tab, or use Clear all data to discard them. Your original files and CSVs you download remain on your device.

    GitHub serves the app and may log normal website requests such as your IP address. Selected file contents are never sent to GitHub. Opening an Instagram profile is optional and takes you to Instagram.

    Don’t upload exports into a GitHub repository. Keep any exported CSV private too.
